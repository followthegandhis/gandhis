Educational Context

Yesterday IT was the hero (+2.88%). Today it's the villain (-1.40%). That's sector rotation at its most violent. Only two defensive sectors survived — Pharma and PSU Bank. When only defensives hold, the market is in risk-off mode. Money isn't entering the market, it's hiding within it.

Two stories. Bajaj Finance found buyers after yesterday's sharp fall — classic dead-cat bounce or genuine support? Eternal reported 92% profit growth but the stock fell — market is rewarding value over growth.

Bajaj Finance bouncing +1.74% after -3.5% is a technical recovery, not a trend change. Eternal falling despite 92% profit growth shows the market is punishing growth stories and preferring value. When good earnings can't lift a stock, sentiment is deeply negative.

Why Breadth Matters

Breadth "improved" from 79% negative to 69% negative — that's still deeply unhealthy. VIX at 15.81 is the highest in 4 months. The options market is pricing in continued turbulence. When breadth stays negative for multiple days, it signals institutional distribution — large players systematically reducing exposure.

FII selling intensified from yesterday's ₹413 Cr to ₹3,459 Cr. The net short position of 2,77,628 contracts shows a confident bearish stance from foreign money.

The Pattern

DIIs have absorbed 107% of FII selling this month — ₹55,875 Cr bought vs ₹52,354 Cr sold. Without DII buying, Nifty would be significantly lower. FIIs have sold in 14 of 16 sessions this October. The net short position of 2,77,628 contracts means FIIs are betting on further downside through derivatives too.

The Context
Brent $111.71, Rupee ₹94.81, US 10Y 5.015%, Gold $4,065 — Crude Is Crushing Everything
Brent crude at $111.71 is the market's primary concern. India imports 85% of its oil — elevated crude threatens wider trade deficits and corporate margins. The rupee weakened to ₹94.81, accelerating FII outflows.
Brent Crude$111.71 (▲ 0.64%)
USD/INR₹94.81 (▲ 0.20%)
US 10Y Yield5.015% (▲ 0.26%)
Gold$4,065 (▼ 2.03%)
Why These Numbers Matter

Crude at $111.71 with India importing 85% of its oil = wider current account deficit. Rupee at ₹94.81 = FII dollar returns erode, accelerating outflows. US 10Y above 5% = money flows to US bonds instead of emerging markets. Gold falling 2.03% = investors selling everything for cash — a liquidity crunch signal. All four macro forces are working against Indian equities simultaneously.

What is Gandhis?

Gandhis is a daily post-market educational guide for the Indian stock market. Every trading day at 8 PM, we publish 5 cards — which sectors moved, what institutions did, and the macro picture.

We go through data from NSE, BSE, and official sources so you don't have to. Five cards. Ninety seconds. That's it.

The 5 Cards — Every Day

Where the Money Moved — which sectors saw capital flow in or out
The Signal — the one earnings result or stock move that stood out
The Data Point — market breadth, VIX, and numbers beneath the surface
The Flow — FII and DII activity, who's buying, who's selling
The Context — crude, rupee, US yields, and global forces

We don't predict the market. We don't recommend stocks. We don't sell tips. We present publicly available data so patterns become visible.

How We Think

We focus on sector rotation — money doesn't leave the market, it moves. Most people look at the Nifty and react. We look at where the money is actually going.
