Educational Context

IT went from best sector (+2.88%) on Wednesday to worst sector (-1.20%) on Thursday. That's the speed of rotation right now. PSU Bank was the only survivor — defensive positioning in state-owned banks when everything else is falling. Crude at $116.72 is the highest since April 2022. India imports 85% of its oil — every $10 rise adds ~0.4% to inflation.

The Signal
Infosys Cut FY27 Revenue Growth Guidance to 1-2%. Stock Fell. IT Sector Reversed Sharply.
Infosys reported higher quarterly revenue but slashed its full-year constant-currency growth outlook from 1.5-3% to just 1-2%. The stock fell and dragged the entire IT sector down. It also declared an interim dividend of ₹26 per share.

What the Data Shows

When the sector leader cuts guidance, the entire sector pays the price. IT went from hero to zero in 24 hours. The guidance cut signals global demand weakness — clients are cutting discretionary tech spending as interest rates stay elevated. This is a leading indicator for the broader economy.

Why These Numbers Matter

Crude at $116.72 is the single biggest threat to Indian markets right now. Houthi strikes on Saudi airports and oil facilities are escalating. Trump hinted at "finishing off" Iran — any military escalation could push crude past $120. Gold falling below $4,000 shows investors are selling everything for cash — a classic liquidity crunch signal. The Nasdaq had its worst day since June on AI spending fears. This week's Jio IPO allotment (Monday) and Fed meeting (Wednesday) are the next catalysts.

What is Gandhis?

Gandhis is a daily post-market educational guide for the Indian stock market. Every trading day at 8 PM, we publish 5 cards — which sectors moved, what institutions did, and the macro picture.

We go through data from NSE, BSE, and official sources so you don't have to. Five cards. Ninety seconds. That's it.

The 5 Cards — Every Day

Where the Money Moved — which sectors saw capital flow in or out
The Signal — the one earnings result or stock move that stood out
The Data Point — market breadth, VIX, and numbers beneath the surface
The Flow — FII and DII activity, who's buying, who's selling
The Context — crude, rupee, US yields, and global forces

We don't predict the market. We don't recommend stocks. We don't sell tips. We present publicly available data so patterns become visible.

How We Think

We focus on sector rotation — money doesn't leave the market, it moves. Most people look at the Nifty and react. We look at where the money is actually going.
