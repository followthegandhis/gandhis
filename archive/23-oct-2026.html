Where the Money Moved
All sectors red except PSU Bank (+0.50%). IT (-1.20%) led the decline as Infosys cut FY27 guidance. Breadth collapsed to 83% negative — worst of the week.
Selling was broad and relentless. IT reversed sharply after Wednesday's +2.88% rally — Infosys cutting FY27 revenue growth guidance to 1-2% from 1.5-3% triggered the selloff. Crude surging to $116.72 (6-month high) crushed sentiment across the board.
Educational Context

IT went from best sector (+2.88%) on Wednesday to worst sector (-1.20%) on Thursday. That's the speed of rotation right now. PSU Bank was the only survivor — defensive positioning in state-owned banks when everything else is falling. Crude at $116.72 is the highest since April 2022. India imports 85% of its oil — every $10 rise adds ~0.4% to inflation.
