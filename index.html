1 Where the Money Moved

Nifty flat on expiry, but the real action was underneath. Media (+3.58%) exploded. Pharma (+1.72%) and Realty (+1.55%) stayed strong. IT (-0.50%) and Private Banks (-0.50%) dragged.

Monthly F&O expiry kept the index range-bound. Nifty opened marginally lower and traded in a narrow 60-point band all day. But broader markets told a different story — Smallcap 100 surged +2.70% and Midcap 100 rose +1.00%. The rotation from large-caps into small and mid-caps accelerated sharply.

What the Data Shows

Tata Consumer touching ₹1,000 is symbolic — the stock was beaten down -3.00% just yesterday. Today's +6.78% reversal shows aggressive dip-buying in consumption names. Bharti Airtel (+3.20%) is now up in 4 of the last 5 sessions — telecom as a defensive play is working. Why Breadth Matters

Nifty moved just 3 points. But 69% of stocks rose — that's a hidden rally beneath a flat index. The 52-week low count dropping from 443 to 157 is the most important number today. It means the selling pressure in the broader market is easing fast. VIX fell 3.5% to 16.53 — back below 17 for the first time since last week. The options market is finally pricing in less fear. Smallcap +2.70% on an expiry day is unusual — it signals genuine buying, not just short-covering.

4 The Flow

FIIs Bought ₹4,240 Cr — Their Biggest Single-Day Purchase in October. DIIs Sold ₹1,250 Cr. Complete Role Reversal on Expiry Day.

For the first time in weeks, FIIs were aggressive buyers. DIIs, who had been absorbing FII selling all month, turned sellers. The roles flipped on monthly expiry — a potentially significant shift.

The Pattern

This is the first time FIIs bought over ₹4,000 Cr in a single day since early October. Out of 18 trading days this month, FIIs sold on 16 and bought on just 2. But today's ₹4,240 Cr is a statement. October FII total improves to -₹52,813 Cr (from -₹57,053 Cr). DII October total adjusts to +₹59,807 Cr. The question: is this a one-day expiry-driven reversal, or the start of FII re-entry? Watch tomorrow's flow — if FIIs buy again, the narrative changes.

What is Gandhis?

Gandhis is a daily post-market educational guide for the Indian stock market. Every trading day at 8 PM, we publish 5 cards — which sectors moved, what institutions did, and the macro picture.

We go through data from NSE, BSE, and official sources so you don't have to. Five cards. Ninety seconds. That's it.

The 5 Cards — Every Day

  • Where the Money Moved — which sectors saw capital flow in or out
  • The Signal — the one earnings result or stock move that stood out
  • The Data Point — market breadth, VIX, and numbers beneath the surface
  • The Flow — FII and DII activity, who's buying, who's selling
  • The Context — crude, rupee, US yields, and global forces

We don't predict the market. We don't recommend stocks. We don't sell tips. We present publicly available data so patterns become visible.
