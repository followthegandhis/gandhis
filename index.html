This is what healthy rotation looks like. When the index is flat but 69% of stocks rise, money isn't leaving — it's moving. Why Breadth Matters

Breadth flipping from 83% negative (Thursday) to 69% positive (Tuesday) is a significant shift. VIX falling 4.72% means options traders are pricing in less fear. But one day doesn't make a trend — the Fed decision on Wednesday will determine if this is a genuine turn or just expiry-day noise. Smallcaps surging 2.7% while Nifty was flat suggests bottom-fishing in beaten-down names.

The Flow
FIIs Sold ₹4,699 Cr — Heaviest Selling Day This Week. DIIs Bought ₹5,182 Cr. October FII Outflow Crosses ₹56,000 Cr.
FIIs dumped ₹4,699 Cr on expiry day — the heaviest single-day selling this week. DIIs countered with ₹5,182 Cr, absorbing 110% of FII selling. October FII outflow now at ₹56,165 Cr — the worst month since March 2020.
FII Cash (27 Oct)-₹4,699 Cr
DII Cash (27 Oct)+₹5,182 Cr
FII Oct Total-₹56,165 Cr
DII Oct Total+₹61,057 Cr
The Pattern

FIIs have sold in 15 of 17 trading days this October. The ₹56,165 Cr outflow is approaching the worst monthly outflow on record. But here's the key: DIIs have absorbed 110% of every rupee FIIs sold. That's why the Nifty is only down ~1% for the month despite record FII selling. The DII wall — powered by SIP flows — is the only thing holding this market together.
